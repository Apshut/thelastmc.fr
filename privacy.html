Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This English version is a translation provided for information only. Only the French version is legally binding.

This page explains what personal data is processed when you use The Last Launcher, The Last Minecraft server and this website, why, for how long, and how to exercise your rights. We collect as little as possible: apart from your IP address, which any server you contact can see, the launcher sends no data about you to The Last, unless you ask it to sign you in to the website (see Your account on the website).

1. Who is responsible?

The Last is an independent project run by a private individual in France. The data controller is the person in charge of The Last project, who can be reached at contact@thelastmc.fr.

2. The launcher

Signing in with your Microsoft account

You sign in through your browser, on Microsoft's official page. The launcher never sees your password. Microsoft sends back an authorization, which the launcher exchanges with Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft to check that you own Minecraft: Java Edition and to retrieve your profile (username, unique identifier and skin).

The launcher has no telemetry, no usage statistics, no advertising and no hardware fingerprinting. It does not send your logs to anyone: the “Export logs” feature creates a file on your computer, which you remain free to share (for example with support).

The services the launcher contacts

As with any Internet connection, each of these services receives your IP address.

  • Microsoft, Xbox Live and Mojang: sign-in, verification that you own the game, profile and skin, downloading Minecraft and Java and, when you sign in to the website, your player certificate and your signed profile. Their processing is governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.
  • Our file server: server content (mods, configuration), news and launcher updates. The host keeps technical logs (IP address, date, file requested) for security purposes. Its name will be given here before launch.
  • Modrinth and the official NeoForge repository: downloading some mods and the mod loader.
  • The Last game server: the launcher queries it to show its status (players online).
  • Our website (api.thelastmc.fr), only when you click “My account on the website”: the launcher proves your identity to the website (see Your account on the website).
  • Discord (optional): if the option is enabled and the Discord app is open on your computer, the launcher tells it that you are playing on The Last, so that it shows on your Discord profile. Can be turned off in the settings.

3. The game server

When you play, the server receives, like any Minecraft server, your username, your unique identifier, your IP address, as well as your messages and your in-game actions. This information is used to run the game, secure it and moderate it (fighting cheating and abuse). Server logs are kept for at most 12 months. Your in-game progress is kept for as long as the server exists, unless you ask for it to be deleted.

4. This website

This website uses no audience measurement tools and no advertising, and loads no third-party services (even the fonts are hosted here), unless you use the Volkar assistant (see section 5) or you have signed in to your account (see Your account on the website). As long as you do not sign in to an account, it sets no cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub receives visitors' IP addresses and keeps them in its security logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

If you write to us, we keep the exchange for as long as it takes to handle your request, then for at most one year.

Your account on the website

Your account is created the first time you sign in from the launcher, with the “My account on the website” button. It is used to show your player head on the website and, once the shop opens, your purchase history. For now, sign-in is limited to a few testers.

How you sign in. The launcher proves that you really are the player using the certificate and the profile that Mojang signed for your account. It sends no password and no Microsoft, Xbox or Minecraft token. Our server checks Mojang's signatures without contacting Mojang; it only downloads your skin image from textures.minecraft.net to cut out the head. Before opening the session, the website shows you your username and your head and asks you “Is this you?”.

Data that has reached the end of its retention period is erased automatically every day. To be able to repair the database in case of failure, Cloudflare also keeps a history that erases itself after 30 days at most; we use it only for a repair, never to recover a deleted account. Our technical logs contain no IP address, no identifier and no username; Cloudflare may keep technical logs under its own rules.

Your username and your head update automatically each time you sign in from the launcher. We make no automated decisions about you and do no profiling: the automatic refusals (too many attempts, different network, access limited to testers) serve only to protect your account.

What the website keeps in your browser. Once you are signed in, a sign-in cookie (__Host-session, restricted to api.thelastmc.fr, unreadable by scripts, valid for 7 days, extended when you come back, 30 days at most) and a marker in local storage (thelast:compte, which is set to “1” and contains no identifier: it keeps the website from querying the server if you have never signed in). They are strictly necessary for the service you request, signing in: they therefore do not require your consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, “loi Informatique et Libertés”). They disappear when you sign out; if your session expires on its own, the marker is erased on your next visit.

Whether you are signed in or not, the website also remembers the language you chose, in local storage (thelast:lang, which is set to “fr” or “en” and nothing else). It stays in your browser until you erase it and is never sent anywhere. When you switch languages, the website also keeps where you were on the page while it reloads (thelast:lang-y, in session storage): it is erased as soon as the page has reloaded.

Who receives them. Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) hosts the account server and its database; we have asked for the database to be located in Western Europe. It acts on our behalf (processor). No one else receives this data.

Your rights, in one click. In My account → Settings, you can download your account data (a JSON file: identifier, username, skin, dates, devices, purchases), sign out all your devices and delete your account. To delete the account, you are asked to have signed in from the launcher within the last 10 minutes, to make sure it really is you. Your Minecraft account is never affected. If you no longer have access to the website, open a private ticket on the Discord or write to us.

5. Volkar, the website's AI assistant

On the home page, the bubble at the bottom right lets you put questions to Volkar, an assistant powered by artificial intelligence (the Claude Haiku model, by Anthropic). So you are talking to an AI, not to a person. It answers solely from the information the team has given it about the server, and it can make mistakes: the terms of use prevail.

  • Why: to answer you, and to protect the service against abuse and excessive costs (the number of questions is limited). We make no automated decisions about you and we do no profiling.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in helping players and protecting the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You are never required to use the assistant: the rest of the website works without it.
  • What data: the text of your question and the beginning of the current conversation, sent with each question; your IP address, which Cloudflare technically sees to route your request; a hash of your IP address, computed with a secret value (we do not keep the IP itself), used only to count your questions. This hash is still personal data, because it makes it possible to recognize the same visitor. Do not write any personal information (name, address, password, age…) in the chat.
  • Who receives it: Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) hosts the program that receives your question; Anthropic PBC (United States) generates the answer from your question and the conversation. They act on our behalf (processors). We do not pass your IP address on to Anthropic.
  • How long: we keep neither your questions nor the answers, and nothing is stored in your browser: the conversation disappears when you close or reload the page. The IP hash is deleted after 2 days at most. According to the rules it publishes as of the date of this page (they may change, and they are what counts), Anthropic states that it deletes messages from its systems within 30 days at most, except content flagged by its safety systems (up to 2 years) or where required by law, and that it does not train its models on content sent through its interface for businesses. Cloudflare may keep technical logs under its own rules.
  • Transfers outside the European Union: Cloudflare and Anthropic are American companies, and the answers are not generated in the European Union. These transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, included in their data processing agreements.
  • Your rights: those in section 8. Since we do not keep your questions, we cannot retrieve a specific conversation; write to us at contact@thelastmc.fr if you have a problem.
  • If you are a minor: never give personal information to Volkar, and ask a parent for advice if you are unsure. For an account problem or a report, open a private ticket on the Discord instead.

6. On what legal basis?

  • Providing the service you request (installing and launching the game, signing you in, letting you play): performance of the terms of use (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Your account on the website (recognizing you, showing your head, your devices and, later, your purchases): performance of the terms of use (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Security and moderation (technical logs, server logs, attempt limits and same-network check when signing in to the website, list of your devices): our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its players (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Answering your questions through the Volkar assistant and protecting it from abuse: our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for commercial purposes.

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Microsoft and GitHub are American companies. Their data transfers to the United States rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on their own safeguards. For Cloudflare and Anthropic (Volkar assistant), see section 5. We have asked for the account database to be located in Western Europe; since Cloudflare is an American company, a transfer to the United States remains possible, governed by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

8. Your rights

You can request access to your data, its rectification, its erasure, the restriction of processing, object to it, and request its portability, by writing to contact@thelastmc.fr. We reply within one month. If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

Your right to object. You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest (section 6): write to us, and we will stop, unless there is a compelling reason, for example the safety of other players.

For your account on the website, export and deletion can also be done directly in My account → Settings (see Your account on the website).

To erase what the launcher keeps on your computer:

  • remove the account in the launcher (Comptes → Retirer, or Accounts → Remove in English): its token is deleted;
  • uninstall the launcher, then delete its data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\fr.thelast.launcher on Windows);
  • you can also revoke access for “The Last Launcher” from your Microsoft account: account.live.com/consent/Manage.

9. Minors

Minecraft is played by many young people. Children's Microsoft accounts are managed by their parents (Microsoft Family). A parent can write to us with any question or to have the data linked to their child's account deleted.

If you are under 15, ask a parent for permission before signing in to the website. A parent can delete the account on the website from My account → Settings, or write to us. If online play is blocked by parental controls, signing in to the website does not work: this is normal.

10. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change; the date at the top of the page shows when. In the event of a significant change, we will announce it in the launcher's news.
