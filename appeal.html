Appeal a sanction

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This English version is a translation provided for information only. Only the French version is legally binding.

This page is for you if you were banned or kicked from The Last's Discord server, by the team or automatically by our bot Volkar. It names nobody and lists nobody.

1. Who is it for?

  • You were banned or kicked from The Last's Discord. You can no longer open a ticket: we get in touch by email.
  • Received a warning? The /mon-casier (my record) command shows your records and lets you appeal, or open a “Contester une sanction” (Appeal a sanction) ticket. In timeout, you cannot write any more: email contact@thelastmc.fr, or open the ticket once the timeout is over.
  • You have 90 days after the sanction to write to us.

2. How to appeal

Write to contact@thelastmc.fr and tell us:

  • your Discord ID (or, failing that, your username);
  • the approximate date of the sanction;
  • what happened, and why you think it is a mistake. A few sentences are enough.

We ask for no ID, no age and no parental consent. If you are a minor, you can write yourself.

3. What happens next

  • We acknowledge receipt within 72 hours.
  • A person answers you within 7 days (30 days at most), with the decision and its reason. If possible, it is not the person who confirmed the decision. They can lift the sanction.
  • An automatic ban lasts 7 days at most: a team member reviews it within 72 hours, and without that review, it is lifted automatically. They can lift it at any time, and only a person can make it permanent.
  • If you were kicked, you can come back with a new invitation. A kick during a wave of fake accounts is not a sanction: it counts no point.

4. How we know it is you

By a means you already have: write to us from the same address for the rest of the exchange, or answer a question about a fact only you know. We never ask for an ID.

5. What Volkar writes to you, and what it does not

Volkar writes to you only once, at the moment of a ban, with no link and no request. If anyone asks you to open a link, or for a password or a code “to appeal”, it is not us. For a kick, the explanation waits for you in a private thread on the server when you come back.

6. What it does not affect

A Discord ban does not affect your website account, your Tavern purchases or your access to the game.

7. Your data

Your message is kept while we handle your request, then for one year at most. The details of what Volkar reads, keeps and erases are in the privacy policy.
